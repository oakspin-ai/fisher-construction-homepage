No surprises, from the first budget to the final invoice.

A large custom home takes time to build. Here is how we keep you informed at every stage.

“The communication throughout the project was also exceptional as there were no surprises during the two year construction period.”

Michael Crawford, President & CEO, Sukut Construction, Inc.

  1. Budget before drawings are final

    We prepare an initial project budget and meet with you, your architect and your designer to explore cost-effective options and alternatives.

  2. Three bids per trade

    Once drawings are complete, we get three bids for every trade, review each one with you and give our recommendation.

  3. Daily and weekly reports

    A full-time project manager runs the site. Daily reports cover the work done and what’s next; weekly reports add digital photographs.

  4. Bi-weekly billing you can read

    Every bill comes with a spreadsheet showing each trade’s forecast budget, contracted amount, percentage billed and remaining balance. Weekly meetings bring the owner, architect, designer and consultants together.

  5. Walkthrough and closeout binder

    We walk the finished home with you and handle any item immediately. You receive a binder with every subcontractor and supplier, plus all warranty cards and manuals.
